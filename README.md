# Cold-Lead Revival Lab

An interactive demo: watch an AI follow-up sequence revive a 94-day-dead lead and re-book the appointment — in under 90 seconds.

- **Live demo:** https://cold-lead-revival-lab-mr-bilal-alis-projects.vercel.app
- **Book a discovery call:** https://cal.com/mrbilalali/discovery-call
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/mrbilalali

## What it shows

A visitor starts from a dead CRM card (a fictional lead, cold for 94 days), taps **Run AI re-engagement**, picks one of three follow-up angles (friendly check-in / seasonal nudge / social proof), sees the AI's personalized draft, and sends it. The lead replies — the visitor picks one of three reply scenarios (ready to re-book, asks for a day with a slot picker, or opt-out) — and the AI shows its intent reading plus a draft response. Positive paths end on a confirmed re-booked appointment; the opt-out path ends on a respectful close with do-not-contact and compliance CRM updates. Every path shows a live revival timer, a Start-over replay, and a discovery-call CTA right after the payoff.

## Honest framing

This is a **scripted simulation**. No real messages are sent, no real calls are placed, and all names, numbers, and events are fictional. Labels to that effect appear at the top of the demo, at every send step, and in the footer.
